# Cross-role attribution for TTimes evaluations

Use when a video separately credits `자료: A` and `편집: B`.

- `자료: A` directly supports attribution of material planning: source/object selection, explanatory structure, and the intended evidence/context/comparison function.
- `편집: B` directly supports attribution of implementation: actual timeline placement, cutting, pacing, assembly, and visible realization.
- Speech-to-visual synchronization can be a joint output of planning and implementation. Attribute it jointly unless project files or explicit credits prove the split.
- Do not assign emphasis-caption authorship to either role from `자료` or `편집` alone. Require an explicit `자막`, `강조자막`, or equivalent credit.
- A video-level quality score can assess the finished output, but it must not be reported as an individual editor score when its dimensions include material planning owned by another credited person.
- Before comparing people or recurring production combinations, preserve every exact credit line and label which observed dimension is individual, joint, or unassignable.
