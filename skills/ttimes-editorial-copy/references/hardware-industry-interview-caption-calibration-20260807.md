# Hardware-industry interview caption calibration — 2026-08-07

Use this reference when an interview question moves from a long market-history setup into a short `질문자막`, or when the emphasis line depends on a preceding contrast.

## 1. Preserve the setup’s editorial predicate

Source progression:

```text
Japan led MLCC and package substrates for years
→ the AI era opened a new market
→ Samsung Electro-Mechanics rose prominently
→ what explains that rise?
```

Rejected frames:

```text
Q. 삼성전기가 AI 시대에 주목받는 이유는?
```

This weakens `급부상/우뚝 섬` into generic attention.

```text
Q. 삼성전기가 FC-BGA 강자로 올라선 비결은?
```

This narrows the subject to a nearby product even though the spoken question is about the company’s broader AI-era rise.

User-confirmed frame:

```text
Q. 삼성전기가 AI 시대에 급부상하게 된 비결은?
```

Rule: preserve the setup’s time frame, subject, and evaluative predicate when they define the question. Do not replace `급부상` with `주목` or insert an adjacent product unless the final interrogative names that product.

## 2. Re-read the full setup after rejection

When the PD says `아닌 것 같아`, do not revise from the rejected caption alone. Re-read the preceding market-history setup, identify the hinge, and rebuild around:

```text
who
in what transition or era
what changed in status
what the guest must explain
```

## 3. Name the technical scope when the spoken question is otherwise vague

If the conversation has explicitly established two categories and the interviewer asks only `중국의 기술 수준은 어디까지 왔나`, a concise question caption may restore that scope:

```text
Q. 중국의 MLCC·기판 기술은 어디까지 왔나?
```

Do not broaden this to generic `전자부품 기술` when the established categories are available.

## 4. Emphasis captions may require the preceding contrast

Source progression:

```text
China uses basic materials such as rare earths to enter the market
→ but still shows a gap in platforms built through advanced products and advanced customers
```

A caption based only on the final sentence loses the market-entry contrast. Preferred two-line frame:

```text
원재료로 시장 진입 노리는 중국
선단 제품·고객 플랫폼은 아직 격차
```

Rule: for `대신에/하지만` beats, include the minimum preceding proposition needed to make the contrast legible. Preserve the speaker’s limited confidence (`아직 격차가 보임`) rather than escalating it into permanent inferiority or total technological backwardness.
