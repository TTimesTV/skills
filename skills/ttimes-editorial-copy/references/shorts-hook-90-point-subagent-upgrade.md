# Technical Shorts Hook Upgrade: 80 → 90+

Use when a technically sound 45–75 second TTimes script scores around 80 because the hook is explanatory, the physical reversal arrives late, or the user explicitly asks for subagent improvement.

## Failure signature

- Opening starts with background such as market need, industry history, or technology classification.
- The strongest sentence appears after 20–30 seconds.
- Factual caveats are correct but occupy the narrative center.
- A named company and shipment target interrupt the mechanism story.
- The script explains why the technology matters before creating a puzzle the viewer wants resolved.

## Target timing

```text
0–3s   conflict or paradox
4–6s   reveal the object
7–15s  physical cause and disadvantage
≤15s   central reversal
15–40s evaluation criteria change
40–52s cost/trade-off or anti-hype qualification
52–65s opening problem recalled in a stronger final line
```

For the sodium-ion ESS class, the durable hook pattern is not `sodium is abundant`. It is:

```text
property penalized in a moving product
→ what if the product never moves?
→ the penalty becomes relatively smaller, not zero
→ the evaluation scorecard changes
```

Do not explain lower energy density as merely “sodium atoms are heavier.” Use the safer mechanism-level result: present commercial sodium-ion generally stores less energy per unit mass or volume than lithium-based competitors, so the same kWh can require a larger/heavier system.

## Subagent fan-out

When the user explicitly asks for subagents, dispatch independent roles on one frozen source/fact brief:

1. **Hook specialist** — one best hook and first 15 seconds, no option dump.
2. **Retention rewriter** — one complete 55–65 second script.
3. **TTimes PD/scorer** — 100-point score with hook and retention weighted heavily.
4. **Adversarial reviewer** — attack overclaim, logic gaps, and dullness; then produce one safe script.

Main must wait for all requested roles to return before calling anything the integrated 90-point version. Do not send a provisional script as final while reviewers are still running. Integrate disagreements by preserving the strongest physical hook and the most conservative factual strength.

## 90-point gate

Score five axes at 20 points each:

- Hook: immediate conflict, no explanatory preamble.
- Structure: problem → physical cause → reversal → trade-off → callback.
- Comprehension: a general viewer understands the object and why location changes its value.
- Retention: strongest reversal by 15 seconds; no catalog/list section.
- Fact safety: potential is not rewritten as proven superiority or commercial operation.

A script is not 90+ if hook or retention is below 17/20, regardless of factual quality.

## What to remove first

- commodity abundance as the first line;
- periodic-table or chemistry history;
- broad market-size forecasts;
- company partnership quantities that are not needed for the causal story;
- full advantage lists (`cost, safety, low temperature, cycle life, recycling`);
- repeated caveats that can be represented once as `scheduled/target`.

Use a named company only when it proves current relevance. If included, keep `product announcement`, `first delivery scheduled`, `shipment target`, and `operating commercial record` distinct.
