# Korean enterprise-AI emphasis-caption calibration — 2026-08-03

Use this reference for TTimes interview beats about enterprise AI rollout, employee adoption, industry usage gaps, MCP, and token consumption.

## Confirmed editorial pattern

When the PD says `이런 식으로` and supplies a two-line skeleton, treat its **relationship and syntax as authoritative**. Make only the smallest legibility fix; do not replace it with a metaphor, moral judgment, or newly inferred consequence.

### Token-use beat

Source meaning:

- even the same user question can consume more tokens
- the speaker ties the increase to producing a higher-quality answer

Rejected framing:

```text
같은 질문에도 늘어나는 토큰
양질의 답변을 위한 불가피한 대가
```

Why rejected:

- `대가` adds a negative cost metaphor
- the source relationship is a simple paired increase, not a moral or economic judgment
- it drops the PD's preferred mirrored rhythm

PD-directed framing:

```text
높아지는 답변 퀄리티만큼
토큰 소모량도 증가
```

Rule: when the source and PD both frame two quantities as rising together, prefer a compact mirrored construction (`A만큼 / B도 증가`) over `원인 / 대가`, `비용`, or `불가피` unless those evaluative words are explicitly requested.

## Sequential enterprise-adoption beats

Keep each beat distinct rather than collapsing the sequence into generic `AI 활용 확대`:

1. **Approval latency** — enterprise solution adoption decisions take a long time.
2. **Post-approval loosening** — after the internal process is passed, usage constraints appear to loosen; preserve the speaker's tentativeness.
3. **Rollout–usage gap** — company-wide rollout does not mean every employee actually uses the tool.
4. **Work-context gap** — organizations with fewer desk/PC-based workers may show relatively lower average usage.

Calibrated copy:

```text
전사 도입이 곧
전 직원의 활용은 아니다
```

```text
PC 업무 인력이 적을수록
평균 AI 사용률도 상대적으로 낮게
```

Pitfalls:

- Do not upgrade `좀 풀어주시는 것 같아요` into `자유롭게 활용` or unrestricted access.
- Do not attach an employee-level conclusion to an enterprise-level adoption statistic.
- Do not generalize construction or manufacturing as inherently low-tech; the source mechanism is the share of employees working at a computer.
- For adjacent official statistics, separate direct evidence from proxies. KOSIS enterprise AI-use tables can support industry/size adoption gaps, but not token consumption or within-company active-user rates.

## MCP copy calibration

For an explainer, prefer one line that names both connection and operation:

```text
MCP(Model Context Protocol) : AI를 사내 데이터·업무 시스템과 연결해 필요한 정보를 가져오고 실제 작업까지 수행하게 하는 표준 연결 방식
```

For an emphasis beat following the explainer, preserve the contrast between unsupported generic output and grounded answers without overclaiming that hallucinations disappear:

```text
‘아무 말 대잔치’에서
데이터에 근거한 양질의 답변으로
```
