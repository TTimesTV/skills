## Application

- Resolve each episode's actual personnel from the production schedule, producer-provided credits, or the same video's approved credits, then apply the name/title label here.
- Preserve the entire approved label; do not drop affiliation or professional title to output only a name.
- A missing person is not permission to guess a title. Use a verified episode source or obtain the needed wording and add the user's confirmed label here.
- CMS internal authors are deduplicated internal staff only, with the same labels as the credits. External presenters such as 박영선 and 이중학 do not enter that internal-author list.
- Do not relabel every contributor as PD, 기자 or 영상디자이너 based only on their task.
