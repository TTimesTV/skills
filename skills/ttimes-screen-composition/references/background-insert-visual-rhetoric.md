# Background Inserts as Visual Rhetoric

Use this reference when the PD chooses footage for the *impression* it creates rather than for literal semantic correspondence.

## Core distinction

- `PROVE`: viewer should read or recognize the evidence; visible content must support the claim.
- `EXPLAIN`: visual structure teaches the concept; correspondence should be reasonably direct.
- `ILLUSTRATE/ATMOSPHERE`: narration carries meaning; footage supplies scale, complexity, pace, mood, or institutional texture.

Do not upgrade an atmospheric insert into evidence merely because it comes from the company being discussed.

## Dense enterprise UI pattern

A dense, orderly software interface can make a business feel operationally complex through:

- simultaneous tables, charts, filters, tabs, model versions, status indicators, and controls;
- visible comparison and evaluation activity;
- a credible enterprise-product aesthetic rather than a generic hacker screen;
- motion such as charts loading, panels updating, or states changing;
- high information density that viewers feel without needing to read.

The visible metrics do **not** need to map one-to-one to nouns in the speech. For example, a line listing products, customers, refunds, permissions, rules, and states may be supported by a model-evaluation dashboard if the intended takeaway is “many interdependent variables are being managed.” Do not claim the dashboard literally models refunds or permissions unless the UI or source says so.

## Selection questions

1. What should the viewer *feel* here: complexity, scale, speed, risk, control, fragmentation, or order?
2. Is the interface dense but visually organized?
3. Can the viewer understand the intended impression without reading small text?
4. Does it avoid locking the narration to an overly narrow industry example?
5. Is there enough motion to function as footage rather than a static screenshot?
6. Can spoken captions remain legible without explanatory overlays?

## Default composition

```text
story_function: ILLUSTRATE/ATMOSPHERE
spoken_caption: KEEP
editorial_caption: NONE unless a separate factual claim is intended
proof_burden: N/A
source_note: identify the official product/video, but do not describe unrelated UI details as evidence
```

## Common failure

Wrong approach: search each noun in the speech and assemble literal matching shots.

Better approach: identify the dominant visual need first. When the line accumulates many organizational entities and rules, use a coherent, dense operational interface to communicate the aggregate complexity. Literal shots are optional, not mandatory.
